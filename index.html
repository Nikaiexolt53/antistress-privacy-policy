Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 24, 2024

Application: 100+ Antistress & Fidget Toys (com.fidgettoys.antistress.asmr.app)

1. Overview

Welcome to 100+ Antistress & Fidget Toys ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how our application operates, what minimal data may be collected by third-party services, and how your preferences are stored.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Our application does NOT require account creation, registration, or login. We do NOT collect, store, or transmit your name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts, photos, or location data to any external server managed by us.

3. Local Storage on Device

To provide a seamless user experience, our app stores non-personal preferences locally on your device using SharedPreferences:

  • Audio & Vibration Settings: Enabling or disabling sound effects and tactile haptics.
  • App Theme: System Default, Light, or Dark mode preference.
  • Selected Language: Your language choice (English, Ukrainian, Spanish, German, French, Polish, Portuguese).
  • Game Progress & Rewards: Stars balance and unlocked fidget toys.

This data stays 100% on your local device and is erased if you uninstall the app or clear app data.

4. Advertising & Third-Party Services

Our application integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to serve advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and process anonymous technical identifiers to serve relevant ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud:

  • Advertising ID (AAID / IDFA)
  • Device model, IP address, and OS version
  • Coarse network location and performance metrics

For more details on Google's privacy practices, please visit Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our application is designed for general audiences and families seeking relaxation and stress relief. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. AdMob is configured to comply with Google Play Families Policies and applicable child privacy regulations.

6. Security

Because we do not store personal data on remote servers, your personal data risk is minimized. All local app preferences are stored securely within Android's protected application sandbox.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted within the app or on our Google Play Store listing page with a revised effective date.
